# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers with many coding-agent checkouts, under urgent disk pressure. They need to understand meaningful savings and approve narrowly scoped actions without interpreting Git internals.

## Product Purpose

An approval-first, standalone disk steward. Find large opportunities, distinguish regenerable artifacts from valuable work, and preserve verified recovery before removing a checkout. Success is measured free space, not an inflated estimate.

## Brand Personality

Calm, compact, trustworthy. Plain language and truthful progress, not a diagnostic dump.

## Anti-references

Long hexadecimal identifiers and absolute paths dominating every row; raw internal blocker codes; reports implying that blocked space is reclaimable; full-screen interfaces required for basic use.

## Design Principles

- Biggest useful savings first.
- Separate cache cleanup from whole-checkout removal.
- Make risks and the exact approval scope unmistakable.
- Keep details available on demand rather than repeating internals.
- Enter skips; quit, EOF and Ctrl+C stop review without further actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use terminal-native text, no color-only meaning, no required animation, readable narrow-terminal layouts and stable JSON for integrations.
